Case notes

  1. 1
    COS 2.7, 2.8 Find the other player by username. You can’t trade with yourself, and you need 3 finished games.
  2. 2
    COS 2.7, 2.8 Up to 500 XP from the sender, never leaving a balance below 0. Free default items can’t be traded.
  3. 3
    COS 2.7 Both users see exactly what each side gives and gets before anything is final. Offers expire after 24 hours, and the sender can cancel.
  4. 4
    COS 2.13, Desirable A counter-offer replaces the original. Traded items can’t be traded again for 24 hours.
  5. 5
    COS 2.8 On accept, the server re-checks ownership and balances and swaps everything at once, or fails with a clear message and changes nothing.
